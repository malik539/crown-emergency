Can I Wait Until I See a Dentist?

It is important that you see a dentist right away in an emergency situation and do not wait to be treated. If you leave a problem untreated, it is likely to get much worse, causing you significantly more pain and end up requiring more expensive treatment.

For any emergency situation, call Crown Dental Care right away to request a same-day appointment.

Knocked out a tooth? Act fast.

If you have knocked out a tooth, it is possible that it may be able to be saved, as long as the root is intact. The sooner you come to see us at Crown Dental Care, the more likely it is that your tooth can be successfully placed back into its socket.
